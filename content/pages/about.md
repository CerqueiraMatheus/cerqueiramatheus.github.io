My full name is Matheus Henrique de Cerqueira Pinto. I'm a PhD student in AI/ML/NLP at the University of Sao Paulo (USP), in Sao Carlos, Brazil, and a Software Engineer at Microsoft.

I've been passionate about computers since childhood. In 2016 I started studying formally as a Computer Technician at the Federal Institute of Parana (IFPR) in Jacarezinho. During four years there I taught robotics, wrote articles, developed websites and apps, and was a finalist at Harvard's VTRAC 2019.

In 2020 I enrolled in Computer Science at USP, the largest university in Latin America. Since then I've worked on topics ranging from data structures and mobile development to machine learning applied to cancer case prediction and named entity recognition in Portuguese.
